# Quality Rubric

Score each dimension from 0-2. A real draft requires no critical failure and at least 16/20.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reader promise | absent | broad | specific and fulfilled |
| Evidence traceability | unsupported claims | partial attribution | claims tied to evidence and scope |
| Paper specificity | generic topic tutorial | some paper details | reconstructs the paper's argument and decisions |
| Method explanation | summary only | components listed | causal mechanism explained |
| Experiment explanation | headline only | metrics present | setup, baseline, metric, result, scope |
| Figure teaching | absent/decorative | captions only | figures introduced and interpreted |
| Limitations | absent | generic | paper-specific and scoped |
| Structure | confusing | complete but mechanical | coherent narrative |
| Language | hype/translationese | mostly clear | precise and natural |
| Mobile presentation | dense/broken | readable | strong visual rhythm |

## Critical Failures

- Invented number, experiment, author claim, or citation.
- Missing paper link.
- Quantitative result without dataset/metric/baseline context.
- Figure included but not explained.
- Author claim presented as established fact.
- Draft could have been written without reading the original paper.
- A theoretical comparison is presented as an experiment, or an ablation is claimed without a specific row and metric.
- Real publication without user approval.

## Final Questions

- Can a new reader state the paper's problem and core insight?
- Can the reader distinguish evidence from interpretation?
- Does every figure earn its place?
- Are the strongest claims the best-supported claims?
- Would removing any paragraph improve the article?
